Preview · Adaptive icon deliverables

PASSforcare icon, on Android & iOS

Original — marketing’s solid mark, a white disc with the tick cut through it, revealing the purple ground beneath — is confirmed as the direction on both platforms. Light, the thin hollow ring-and-tick, stays on this page for reference and comparison only, not as a live alternative. Both use their source vertex paths unmodified, scaled and centred so the mark sits consistently on each platform’s canvas.

Original (confirmed) solid disc + tick cutout, no stroke to thin out Light (reference) hollow ring + tick, ≈2.4dp stroke Canvas 108×108dp Keyline 66dp Brand purple #6C1B98

Where each mark lands against the brief

Original is confirmed and is a solid shape, not a stroke — the brief's 4dp-minimum-stroke concern doesn't apply to it at all, since there's no hairline to disappear at small sizes. Light is kept here only for reference: it still sits under the brief's 4dp minimum and carries the small-size legibility risk already flagged, one more reason Original is the safer pick.

05 · Small-size legibility

Does the mark hold up when it shrinks?

App-drawer, settings and notification contexts render the icon far smaller than the launcher grid. Original’s solid disc and cutout stay crisp all the way down; Light’s hollow ring and tick thin out noticeably below 28–36px — one of the reasons Original is the confirmed pick.

Same risk, different platform

iOS has no equivalent to the brief's 4dp minimum — that's an Android adaptive-icon spec — but the underlying problem carries over identically. Original, confirmed, stays legible at every size iOS renders it. Light is kept only for reference: its thin stroke is the one to watch, especially at Settings/notification sizes.

00 · Icon shape

One shape, system-applied

iOS has no adaptive-icon layering and no launcher-dependent mask. You supply one flat, fully opaque square; the OS applies its own fixed continuous-corner ("squircle") mask at render time — the same corner curve everywhere, on every device. Don’t bake rounded corners, shadows or transparency into the artwork yourself.

02 · Appearance modes

Light & Dark appearance

iOS 18 lets a user choose Light or Dark for every app icon on their Home Screen, independent of system theme. (Apple also offers a system Tinted mode, but that’s not something we’re designing for here.) Light’s thin stroke is still the one to watch at small sizes, same risk flagged for Android.

03 · App Store listing

1024×1024, no alpha, single size

Since Xcode 14, a single 1024×1024 PNG (no alpha channel, no corner rounding) is enough — Xcode and App Store Connect generate every smaller size from it. Apple applies its own mask on the product page, same principle as Android’s Play Store squircle.
