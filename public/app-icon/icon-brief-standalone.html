Design brief · Android launcher icons

PASSforcare adaptive icons

Our app icon renders noticeably smaller than every neighbour on the home screen. The cause is an asset gap, not a bug — we never shipped an adaptive icon. This is what design needs to produce to close it.

Repo pass-android · :app App PASSforcare minSdk 31 Variants affected all 6

Cause

The launcher is shrinking our icon, and it is allowed to

PASSforcare ships only legacy square bitmaps — one PNG per screen density, no adaptive icon. Since Android 8.0, launchers mask every icon to a device shape (circle on Pixel). A square bitmap cannot be masked without slicing the corners off the artwork, so the launcher falls back to legacy icon normalisation: it measures the visible art, scales it down until it fits entirely inside the mask, and paints a generated white circle behind it.

An adaptive icon skips all of that. Its foreground is drawn at the full tile size and the mask simply cuts the background layer. That is why Phone, Photos and Play Store fill their circles edge to edge while we sit shrunken inside ours.

The canvas

Every layer is drawn on a 108×108dp artboard

An adaptive icon is built from stacked full-bleed layers rather than one finished picture. Each layer fills the whole 108dp artboard; the system decides what the user sees by masking it. Only the centre is guaranteed to survive.

108×108dp — full canvas
Both foreground and background must fill this completely. No baked rounded corners, no baked circle, no drop shadow — the system supplies the shape.
Outer 18dp on all four sides — bleed
Cropped by the mask, and shifted during the launcher’s parallax animation. Background colour and texture only. Nothing readable here.
Centre 72×72dp — safe zone
Always visible on every mask shape. The tick, the ring and the variant badge all live inside this.
66dp keyline
Google’s optical sizing target. A circular mark is drawn to 66dp diameter; a square mark to 60dp. Ours is circular — use 66dp.

Deliverables

Three layers per variant, plus one store asset

Supply everything as SVG. The artwork is line-based, so it converts cleanly to Android’s vector format and we stop maintaining five PNG sizes per variant.

Asset Format Canvas Rules
ic_launcher_foreground SVG 108 × 108dp Transparent ground. Ring, tick and badge inside the 72dp safe zone, mark drawn to the 66dp keyline.
ic_launcher_background Hex value, or SVG 108 × 108dp Fully opaque, edge to edge. A flat brand colour is enough — send the hex and we skip the file.
ic_launcher_monochrome SVG 108 × 108dp Silhouette only, one flat colour, transparent elsewhere, no background layer. The system recolours it for themed icons. Must still read as us with all colour removed.
ic_launcher-web.png PNG, 32-bit 512 × 512px Play Store listing. Square, no alpha channel, no rounded corners, no shadow — Play applies its own. Production build only.

Not needed

Do not export density PNG sets (mdpi through xxxhdpi). Our minimum supported Android version is 12, so every device we ship to reads adaptive icons. The existing bitmaps get deleted, which also clears two size inconsistencies already in the repo.

Art direction

Four things to change while redrawing

The strokes are too thin to survive a launcher

The ring and tick are hairlines. At the size a launcher actually draws an icon they lose weight, and on the themed-icon treatment they nearly vanish. Redraw at a minimum 4dp stroke on the 108dp canvas.

measured: ring 4px on a 192px asset = 1dp · tick = 1.5dp

White artwork on a white launcher circle has no presence

Production, QA, QA-app and Pen are purple line art on a white square. Once the launcher supplies its own white circle behind it, the icon reads as a floating hairline with no brand colour in the grid at all. Recommendation: purple background layer with the mark reversed out in white — the treatment the internal builds already use.

Variant badges sit where the mask cuts

QA, PEN and MOCK are set low and right, about 10% in from the edge. On the adaptive canvas that lands in the bleed margin and gets clipped on a circular mask. Move each badge fully inside the 72dp safe zone, and set it in outlines — Android’s vector format cannot render live text.

Two purples are in circulation

The line-art icons and the solid-shape icons use different brand purples. Pick one canonical value and apply it across all six variants.

#6C1B98 — line art #733089 — solid shapes

Scope

Six variants, six icon sets

Each build variant carries its own icon so testers can tell environments apart on one device. Every one needs the full three-layer set.

prod

com.elt.passforcare no badge The public icon. Also needs the 512px store asset.

qaapp

com.elt.passforcare.qaapp badge: QA Default development variant. Currently identical artwork to qa.

qa

com.elt.passforcare.qa badge: QA — clashes Indistinguishable from qaapp today. Worth giving these two distinct badges.

pen

com.elt.passforcare.pen badge: PEN Penetration-test builds.

stub

com.elt.passforcare.stub badge: MOCK Mocked-data builds. Uses the solid purple treatment.

demo

com.elt.passforcare shows a QA badge Has no icon of its own, so it inherits the internal fallback — a customer-facing demo build currently installs with a QA-badged icon. Needs a decision: its own mark, or the production one.

Handover

File format constraints

Android converts each SVG into its own vector format, which supports less than SVG does. Anything on this list silently drops or fails to import, so please flatten it at export:

  • Text must be outlined. Badge lettering as paths, not live type.
  • No filters, blurs, masks or clip-paths. Unsupported — bake the result into the path.
  • No gradients on strokes. Fills may carry a linear or radial gradient; strokes may not. Expand the stroke to a filled path if a gradient is needed.
  • Flatten transforms and expand compound shapes into single paths where practical.
  • Name layers exactly as listed in the deliverables table — those become the filenames engineering wires up.

Deliver as one folder per variant: prod / qaapp / qa / pen / stub / demo.

Acceptance

Checks before sign-off

  • Mark fills the 66dp keyline — the icon visually matches the size of Phone, Photos and Play Store in the same grid.
  • Nothing readable falls outside the 72dp safe zone on circle, squircle, rounded-square or teardrop masks.
  • Background layer is opaque to all four edges, with no baked corner radius or shadow.
  • Monochrome layer still reads as PASSforcare with colour removed, in both light and dark themed-icon treatments.
  • Variant badge is legible at a 48dp render, not just at full size.
  • Store PNG is 512×512, square, alpha-free.
  • All six variants are distinguishable from one another on a single device.
